Bio

I am a master's student at the School of Electronic and Information Engineering, Beihang University, and expect to graduate in 2027. My research focuses on high-performance implementations of Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) software receivers. I received my bachelor's degree from the College of Electronic Science and Engineering, Jilin University, in 2024.

My research interests lie in AI infrastructure and high-performance computing, with a particular focus on efficient and scalable systems for large language model training and inference. I am interested in GPU kernel optimization, parallel and distributed computing, and hardware-aware system design to improve the computational efficiency and resource utilization of large-scale AI workloads.

I am seeking PhD opportunities to further develop my research in these areas.
